## Introduction

Singleton Software Development ("Singleton SD", "we," "our," or "us") operates the public
website at [singletonsd.com](https://singletonsd.com) (the "Site"). This notice explains what
information we may collect when you visit the Site, send a contact or waitlist form, or book
a discovery call.

## Information we collect

- **Contact and waitlist details** you submit on the Site: name, email, optional company,
  phone, message, product or feature for waitlists, and whether you opt in to product
  updates. We also store that you consented to this notice (version `ssd-privacy-v1`).
- **Contact details** you share when you email us or book a calendar appointment (for example
  name, email, and meeting notes).
- **Technical data** such as IP address, browser type, and basic request logs from hosting
  providers (GitHub Pages and related infrastructure).
- **CMS editor accounts** for authorised editors who sign in with GitHub to update Site content
  via Decap CMS.

We do not sell personal information. A hidden honeypot field on the forms is used only to
drop automated spam; please leave it blank.

## How we use information

- Respond to enquiries, waitlist sign-ups, and schedule discovery calls
- Operate, secure, and improve the Site
- Send product updates only if you opt in
- Comply with legal obligations

Contact and waitlist forms POST JSON from your browser to
`https://api.singletonsd.com/v1/leads`. Cloudflare Turnstile helps us reject bots.

## Sharing

We may share information with infrastructure providers (for example GitHub, our API gateway,
and the CRM we use to follow up) solely to operate the Site and handle enquiries. We require
processors to protect data appropriately.

## Retention

We keep enquiry and operational logs only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete
or anonymise them.

## Your choices

Contact [support@singletonsd.com](mailto:support@singletonsd.com) to request access, correction,
or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. You can
decline marketing updates; the marketing checkbox is off by default.
